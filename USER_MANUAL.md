## 2. Platform Overview & System Philosophy

The **NSE Options Strategy Intelligence Platform** transforms raw National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) equity derivative data into actionable quantitative intelligence, structured strategy evaluations, real-time risk profiles, and dynamic multi-variable scenario modeling.

### Core Architectural Pillars
1. **Seamless Modularity**: The system functions as a dual-mode terminal:
   - **Option Chain Terminal**: High-density real-time matrix of Call/Put strikes, volume heatmaps, Open Interest buildup/unwinding, and Max Pain settlement curves.
   - **Options Strategy Intelligence**: Quantitative strategy scanner evaluating 16 classical option strategies, dynamic payoff curve builder, pre-expiry scenario heatmaps, and walk-forward backtesting models.
2. **Data Provenance Transparency**: Every visual element, API payload, and stored record displays an explicit data provenance tag:
   - `LIVE`: Originating from an authenticated market-data vendor or broker feed.
   - `HISTORICAL`: Point-in-time historical tick or bar data from licensed archives.
   - `SAMPLE`: Mathematically consistent synthetic data (Black-Scholes generated) used for offline development, calibration, and UI validation.
3. **Adaptive Design System**: Built with an institutional multi-theme engine featuring:
   - **🌸 Pastel Day** (Default light theme optimized for high-brightness trading desks).
   - **🌙 Pastel Dusk** (Twilight slate theme designed to reduce eye strain).
   - **🌑 Classic Dark** (Obsidian dark theme for high-contrast multi-monitor setups).

## 4. Section 1: Option Chain Terminal Guide

### 4.1 Executive Metric Cards
Positioned at the top of the terminal, these cards provide immediate situational awareness:
1. **Underlying Spot**: Real-time spot price and exchange timestamp.
2. **ATM Strike**: At-The-Money strike with point distance to spot.
3. **Max Pain Strike**: Strike price where option writers (sellers) incur the minimum cumulative payout liability at expiration. Often acts as a gravitational settlement magnet.
4. **Put-Call Ratio (PCR)**: Total Put Open Interest divided by Total Call Open Interest.
   - **Bullish**: $\text{PCR} \ge 1.20$ (Heavy put writing forming strong support).
   - **Neutral**: $0.80 < \text{PCR} < 1.20$ (Balanced market positioning).
   - **Bearish**: $\text{PCR} \le 0.80$ (Heavy call writing capping upside).
5. **Total Open Interest Bar**: Relative distribution between cumulative Call OI (Blue) and Put OI (Red).

### 4.2 Key Support & Resistance Banner
Automatically scans the active option chain to call out:
* **Major Support**: Strike price possessing the single highest Put Open Interest.
* **Major Resistance**: Strike price possessing the single highest Call Open Interest.
* **Market Bias Interpretation**: Synthesizes PCR and Max Pain alignment into a single-sentence takeaway.

### 4.3 Controls Bar
* **Expiry Date Selector**: Filter by upcoming weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly derivative expirations.
* **Strikes Range Filter**: Restrict visible strikes to $\pm 5$, $\pm 10$, $\pm 15$, $\pm 25$, or `All` strikes around the ATM strike.
* **Strike Search**: Substring filter to immediately locate a specific strike price.
* **One-Click Export**: Instant download of the active dataset in **CSV**, **Apache Parquet**, or **Raw JSON**.

### 4.4 Option Chain Matrix Table
High-density financial matrix displaying Call Options (CE) on the left, Strike Price in the center, and Put Options (PE) on the right:
* **OI Bar**: Relative visual bar showing Open Interest depth compared to the maximum strike OI.
* **Chg OI**: Intraday net contracts added or unwound. Green indicates net buildup; Red indicates unwinding.
* **IV**: Annualized Implied Volatility calculated from market quotes.
* **LTP & Change**: Last Traded Price and intraday point change.
* **ITM Shading**: In-The-Money options ($S > K$ for Calls, $S < K$ for Puts) feature a subtle highlighted background tint.
* **ATM Strike Row**: The strike closest to the spot price is highlighted with an `ATM` badge and distinctive border.

### 4.5 Non-F&O Cash Equities Fallback
When searching for securities listed exclusively on the NSE Cash Market (e.g. `JPL`), the terminal gracefully displays an active **NSE Cash Equity Status Card** indicating Spot Price, Market Series (`EQ`), Day High/Low, 52-Week Range, and quick-access buttons to return to active F&O leaders (`RELIANCE`, `TCS`, `INFY`, `NIFTY`).

#### Strategy Card Indicators
* **Confidence Score (0-100)**: Transparent algorithmic quality rating:
  - `QUALIFIED` ($\ge 75$): Strong quantitative tailwinds, favorable regime alignment, solid R/R, and narrow execution spreads.
  - `WATCHLIST` ($50 - 74$): Acceptable structure, but may carry moderate IV crush risk or wider spreads.
  - `REJECTED` ($< 50$): Fails key risk hurdle or directly opposes current market regime.
* **Inspect Rules Action**: Opens a dedicated evaluation modal detailing every passed criterion, failed hurdle, and specific invalidation conditions (e.g. exit trigger if underlying drops below major support).
* **Send to Simulator Action**: Transports the strategy's resolved legs into the interactive Payoff Simulator with one click.

### 5.3 Payoff Simulator & Strategy Analysis Sub-Tab
An institutional-grade interactive strategy builder and scenario analyzer:

#### 1. Live Summary Metrics Bar
* **Net Position**: Indicates whether the strategy is a **Net Debit** (cash paid upfront) or **Net Credit** (cash received upfront).
* **Max Profit / Max Loss**: Calculated theoretical payout limits. Displays `Unlimited 🚀` or `Unlimited ⚠️` for naked positions.
* **Breakevens**: High-precision root calculation of exact underlying expiration prices where net PnL is zero.
* **Est. Margin**: Approximate exchange margin requirement (SPAN + Exposure).
* **Total Friction**: Complete estimate of Indian transaction costs including Brokerage (₹20/order), STT (0.1%), Exchange turnover fees (0.05%), GST (18%), Stamp duty, and slippage.
* **Net Greeks**: Portfolio combined Delta ($\Delta$), Gamma ($\Gamma$), Theta ($\Theta$), and Vega ($\nu$).

#### 2. Strategy Legs Builder Table
* **Action Selector**: Toggle between `BUY` and `SELL`.
* **Option Type**: Toggle between `CALL (CE)` and `PUT (PE)`.
* **Strike Price**: Set leg strike in increments of the underlying step (e.g. 50 pts for Nifty).
* **Quantity**: Lot-size increments (e.g. 25, 50, 75).
* **Entry Premium**: Override with custom prices or market quotes.
* **Dynamic Actions**: Click `+ Add Leg` to introduce additional legs or `✕` to remove legs. Click `🔄 Recalculate` to refresh.

#### 3. Expiry Payoff Chart
Interactive Chart.js canvas rendering:
* **Green Fill**: Profit zone above zero.
* **Red Fill**: Loss zone below zero.
* **Breakeven Crossings**: Clear intersection points along the zero horizontal axis.

#### 4. Pre-Expiry Multi-Variable Scenario Matrix Heatmap
A 5x5 simulation matrix modeling pre-expiry PnL (T+5 days) under cross-variable shifts:
* **Columns**: Spot Price movement ($-4\%$, $-2\%$, $0\%$, $+2\%$, $+4\%$).
* **Rows**: Implied Volatility shifts ($-6\%$, $-3\%$, $0\%$, $+3\%$, $+6\%$).
* **Cells**: Green for positive PnL; Red for negative PnL. Essential for stress-testing credit spreads against volatility spikes.

## 7. Troubleshooting & Operational FAQ

### Q1: Why does a stock (e.g. JPL) show "Cash Only" with no option strikes?
**Answer**: Derivatives (F&O) are legally restricted on NSE to ~180 eligible securities approved by SEBI. Non-F&O stocks trade strictly in the **Cash Equity segment (`EQ`)**. The platform automatically detects this and presents real-time cash spot pricing, company metadata, and day ranges without crashing.

### Q2: Why does the top banner say "Data Mode: SAMPLE"?
**Answer**: To protect users from unauthorized website scraping and rate-limiting penalties, the platform defaults to a deterministic Black-Scholes mathematical synthesizer for development and offline testing. To connect a live broker feed, configure licensed broker credentials in `.env` (`OPTIONS_DATA_PROVIDER=broker`).

### Q3: How do I export data for Excel or Python analysis?
**Answer**: On the Option Chain Terminal controls bar, click the **CSV**, **Parquet**, or **JSON** buttons. The active filtered strike matrix and indicators will download immediately to your local system.

### Q4: How do I switch color themes?
**Answer**: Use the theme dropdown in the top navigation header (`🌸 Pastel Day`, `🌙 Pastel Dusk`, `🌑 Classic Dark`). Your preference is automatically stored in `localStorage` and will persist across sessions.
